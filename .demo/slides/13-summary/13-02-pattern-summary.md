## Pattern Selection Summary

| Pattern | Best For | Complexity | Cost | Latency |
|---------|----------|------------|------|---------|
| Prompt Chaining | Sequential, fixed subtasks | Low | Medium | High |
| Routing | Distinct categories | Low | Low-Med | Low |
| Parallelization | Independent/voting tasks | Medium | High | Low |
| Orchestrator-Workers | Dynamic decomposition | Medium | High | High |
| Evaluator-Optimizer | Iterative refinement | Medium | High | High |
| Autonomous Agent | Open-ended problems | High | Highest | Highest |
