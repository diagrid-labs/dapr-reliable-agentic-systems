# What are Agentic Systems?

Agentic systems use LLMs to make decisions and take actions.

## Key Components

- **Reasoning & Planning** - Breaking down tasks, decision making, and strategy formulation
- **Memory** - Context & knowledge management
- **Tools** - Ability to interact with external systems
- **Orchestration** - Coordinating multiple steps

# Agentic Systems ARE Distributed Systems

They involve communication across services (LLMs), message brokers, and data stores.

## Familiar Challenges

- **Network Reliability** - Calls can fail
- **Service Availability** - Dependencies can be down
- **Data Consistency** - State synchronization
- **Fault Tolerance** - Recovering from failures
- **Observability** - Understanding system behavior

## Good News

We've been here before with microservices! Let's apply what we know and use existing concepts and patterns.
